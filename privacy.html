Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Atlas Calendar is a private, single-user application operated by an individual for their own personal use. It has no other users.

Information accessed

With the owner's explicit consent, the application accesses Google Calendar event data using the read-only scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly. No other Google data is requested or accessed.

How the information is used

Calendar events are read so that the owner's personal AI assistant, running on hardware the owner controls, can answer the owner's own questions about their schedule. The data is used for no other purpose.

Storage, sharing, and retention

Calendar data is not stored persistently, not transmitted to any third party, not sold, and not used for advertising or analytics. OAuth credentials are stored only on the owner's own server.

Revoking access

The owner may revoke this application's access at any time via Google Account permissions.
